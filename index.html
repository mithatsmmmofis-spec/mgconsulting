01

How we work

The same four phases apply to every client. Phases, deliverables and dates are agreed in writing before work begins.

Phase 01

Transition and diagnosis

Review of existing records, opening balances and carried-forward amounts, with gaps and exposures documented in writing.

Phase 02

Set-up

Chart of accounts built around the business, document flow and reporting calendar defined, e-document obligations confirmed.

Phase 03

Monthly cycle

Bookkeeping, statutory filings, e-ledger certification and a fixed set of reconciliation controls.

Phase 04

Period close

Valuation and provisioning, checklist close, financial statements and a period review report.

Financial consulting and CFO services

Financial Consulting and CFO Services

MG Consulting provides financial consulting and CFO services that give businesses strategic financial insight and support.

Scope of the service

Financial consulting and CFO services cover the management of a company's finances through regular reporting, planning and compliance.

MG Consulting provides financial consulting and CFO services that give businesses strategic financial insight and support. The work starts with understanding the company's goals and the difficulties it faces; financial solutions specific to the company are then prepared and financial performance is monitored regularly.

The service covers management reporting, accounting and compliance, financial modelling and budgeting, payroll and social security, cash flow management and tax planning.

Reporting

Management reporting

Monthly close, P&L and balance sheet analysis, segment and product profitability, board presentations.

Accounting

Accounting and compliance

Bookkeeping, e-invoice and e-ledger, tax returns, period-end closing, TFRS and BOBİ FRS reporting.

Modelling

Financial modelling and budgeting

Annual budget, scenario and sensitivity analysis, models for investment, pricing and financing decisions.

Payroll

Payroll and social security

Monthly payroll, social security filings and incentives, severance and notice calculations, onboarding and exit procedures.

Cash

Cash flow management

13-week cash forecast, working capital and collection discipline, planning of bank and credit structure.

Tax

Tax planning and company check-up

Tax burden planning, filing calendar, audit readiness, risk screening on the trial balance.

02

Services

Work is delivered across eight service lines. Each line covers the components listed beside it.

A

Accounting and Compliance

Statutory books and the monthly finance cycle, run end to end.

  • Bookkeeping and maintenance of statutory records
  • E-ledger preparation, certification and archiving
  • Preparation and filing of returns and notifications
  • E-invoice, e-archive and e-waybill transitions, scope monitoring
  • Chart of accounts design and remediation of existing records
B

Tax Advisory

Assessment of the tax consequences of transactions, and compliance support.

  • Corporate and personal income tax applications
  • VAT, withholding and VAT refund procedures
  • Exemptions, allowances and investment incentive regimes
  • Transfer pricing and related party transactions
  • Support during tax audits and explanation requests
C

Payroll and HR Administration

Payroll and social security administration.

  • Payroll preparation and payroll outsourcing
  • Social security registrations and monthly declarations
  • Identification and monitoring of employment incentives
  • Severance, notice and annual leave calculations
D

Financial Reporting

Statements and reports for management and external users.

  • Reporting under BOBİ FRS and TFRS
  • Identification of differences between tax rules and standards
  • Deferred tax computation and reconciliation
  • Group reporting and consolidation support
  • Audit readiness and information requests
E

Financial Modelling and Planning

Numbers built for decisions: budgets, forecasts, scenarios and valuation models.

  • Budget and annual plan models
  • Three-statement models linking income statement, balance sheet and cash flow
  • Scenario and sensitivity analysis
  • Investment appraisal, payback and discounted cash flow analysis
  • Financial projections for business plans and credit applications
  • Valuation support and shareholding structure modelling
F

Cash Flow and Working Capital

Where cash comes from, where it goes, and when it runs short.

  • 13-week rolling cash flow forecast
  • Measurement of the cash conversion cycle: receivable, inventory and payable days
  • Working capital requirement analysis
  • Monitoring of bank facilities and covenant headroom
  • Early-warning reporting on cash shortfalls
G

Company Check-Up

A one-off diagnostic review of accounts, tax exposure and process discipline.

  • Review of records and trial balance integrity
  • Tax exposure and non-deductible expense review
  • Reconciliation of filings to the accounting records
  • Ratio analysis and assessment of financial structure
  • Risk-rated report with remediation recommendations
H

Corporate Transactions and Market Entry

Corporate procedures from incorporation through to liquidation.

  • Incorporation, branch and liaison office set-up
  • Foreign investor set-up and reporting obligations
  • Capital increases, share transfers and shareholding changes
  • Support on mergers, transfers and conversions
  • Liquidation and closure procedures
03

Company Check-Up

A one-off, fixed-scope review that can be commissioned without changing your current accountant. The output is a risk-rated report.

Fixed scope · fixed duration

How would your accounts look to an inspector?

The review covers record integrity, tax exposure, payroll compliance, e-document scope and financial structure. Every finding is reported with its basis and a remediation recommendation.

Data can be processed locally where preferred; given professional secrecy and data protection duties, we offer an option that does not require uploading your records.

Review sequence

  1. Collection of trial balance, ledgers and filed returns
  2. Reconciliation controls across VAT, withholding, social security and turnover
  3. Tax exposure and non-deductible expense scan
  4. Ratio analysis and financial structure assessment
  5. Risk-rated report and a review meeting
